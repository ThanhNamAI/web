# TOEIC Mock-Test Design Basis

The mock-test feature models TOEIC Listening & Reading as two timed sections: Listening for 45 minutes and Reading for 75 minutes. ETS confirms the assessment has 100 questions in each timed section and spans Parts 1–4 for Listening and Parts 5–7 for Reading. [1]

| Part | Learning format in TOEIC Quest | Full-test target | Practice design |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Photograph description | 6 | Image prompt with four descriptions |
| 2 | Question–response | 25 | Audio-first with three responses |
| 3 | Conversation | 39 | Dialogue, transcript reveal after submission, three questions |
| 4 | Talk | 30 | Talk, transcript reveal after submission, three questions |
| 5 | Incomplete sentence | 30 | Grammar/vocabulary multiple choice |
| 6 | Text completion | 16 | Short text and context-sensitive blanks |
| 7 | Reading comprehension | 54 | Single/multiple passage questions |

The first release provides a **timed adaptive simulation** whose duration and item count are bounded by available authored content. It labels results as practice simulation, not an official ETS score. Full-test targets follow the public part-by-part format reference. [2]
