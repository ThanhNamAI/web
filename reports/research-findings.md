# Research Findings — TOEIC Quest Upgrade

## TOEIC skill model

ETS describes the TOEIC Listening & Reading assessment as two timed sections of 100 questions. Listening covers Parts 1–4, while Reading covers Parts 5–7. The skill analytics upgrade will retain learning-level metrics for vocabulary and grammar but will aggregate exam readiness primarily into Listening and Reading, with drill-level detail mapped to the relevant TOEIC part. [1]

## Theme accessibility

The CSS `prefers-color-scheme` media feature detects the light/dark preference chosen in the operating system or user agent. The dark-mode upgrade should store an explicit user override while using the operating-system setting as a safe default, and it will keep semantic color tokens rather than hard-coded colors. [2]

## Learning methodology

Research results identified spacing and retrieval practice as evidence-based strategies for durable learning. The product upgrade will keep SRS as the central review mechanism, diversify the recall format, and present specific corrective feedback after practice rather than merely marking answers correct/incorrect. [3]

## Achievement design

Digital badges should represent a specific, criteria-based accomplishment and include the criteria, date and evidence of achievement. The upgrade will therefore favor private mastery badges such as a sustained review routine or a verified skill milestone, rather than a public leaderboard based on raw time or streak length. The streak experience will include recovery-friendly messaging so that a missed day does not turn a learning record into a punitive failure. [4]
