# Full Mock-Test Design Basis

The TOEIC L&R format has 200 multiple-choice questions in two sections: 100 Listening questions in approximately 45 minutes and 100 Reading questions in 75 minutes. Parts 1–4 belong to Listening; Parts 5–7 belong to Reading. [1] [2]

| Section | Parts | Target items | Timer |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Listening | 1–4 | 100 | 45 minutes |
| Reading | 5–7 | 100 | 75 minutes |
| Total | 1–7 | 200 | 120 minutes |

The question bank must contain **original workplace-English practice items**. It must not reproduce ETS directions, sample-test questions, or protected exam material. The product must identify the result as a practice simulation, not an official TOEIC result.
