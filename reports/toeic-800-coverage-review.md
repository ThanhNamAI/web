# TOEIC 800+ Coverage Review

## Finding

The current bank has **1,250 unique learning units**, zero missing IPA entries, and three non-duplicated example variants per unit. Its topical coverage is broad across finance, HR, business, manufacturing, travel, office, technology, customer service, marketing, phrasal verbs and phrases.

There is no official fixed word-count threshold for a TOEIC score of 800+. ETS describes TOEIC Listening & Reading as a workplace English assessment spanning Listening Parts 1–4 and Reading Parts 5–7. Therefore, vocabulary quantity alone is not sufficient; precision in collocations, phrasal verbs, workplace context, and timed listening/reading application must remain part of the learning plan. [1]

## Design decision

Rather than claim that a larger arbitrary word list guarantees 800+, the next content extension will prioritize advanced collocations and multi-word workplace expressions. The 990prep 800+ vocabulary guide reinforces the relevance of finance, HR, logistics/travel, manufacturing, technology, office language, phrasal verbs and high-frequency collocations. [2]

## UX review

The desktop dashboard leads with one primary action, “Bắt đầu buổi học,” then a short sequence of next actions. On mobile, the fixed navigation now presents four essential paths and an explicit “Thêm” control rather than silently hiding Games, Profile and theme controls. The expanded menu exposes every remaining area and the theme switcher. The vocabulary page uses a single search input, scrollable topic chips, a visible result count and a progressive “Tải thêm” control, avoiding an overwhelming all-at-once list.
